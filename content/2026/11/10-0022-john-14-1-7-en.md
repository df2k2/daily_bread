## The Reading (NLT)

**John 14:1-7**

> <sup class="verse-num">1</sup> Don't let your hearts be troubled. Trust in God, and trust also in me. <sup class="verse-num">2</sup> There is more than enough room in my Father's home. If this were not so, would I have told you that I am going to prepare a place for you? <sup class="verse-num">3</sup> When everything is ready, I will come and get you, so that you will always be with me where I am. <sup class="verse-num">4</sup> And you know the way to where I am going. <sup class="verse-num">5</sup> No, we don't know, Lord, Thomas said. We have no idea where you are going, so how can we know the way? <sup class="verse-num">6</sup> Jesus told him, I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one can come to the Father except through me. <sup class="verse-num">7</sup> If you had really known me, you would know who my Father is. From now on, you do know him and have seen him!

## The Story

The atmosphere in the upper room is thick with confusion and grief. Jesus has just washed his disciples' feet, predicted his own betrayal, and told them he is leaving for a destination they cannot immediately reach. Naturally, panic sets in among his closest friends. They are losing their teacher and their sense of direction all at once. Thomas voices the collective anxiety of the group, asking for a clear map or a set of instructions. Instead of giving them geographical coordinates, Jesus offers himself as the very path they are looking for. He assures them that their ultimate destination is a spacious, welcoming reality secured by his own presence.

## Application for Today

We often want a detailed blueprint for our lives, especially when the future feels uncertain or frightening. When transitions loom and familiar structures crumble, our instinct is to demand a step-by-step guide to navigate the unknown. Yet the reassurance offered here is not a rigid itinerary but a relationship. The promise is that we are not left to find our own way through the dark. Instead, following the person of Christ becomes our compass, grounding us in truth and leading us into a life of deep connection with the Divine. We can rest in the knowledge that space has already been intentionally made for us.
