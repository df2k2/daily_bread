## The Story

In the ancient world, clothing was a primary indicator of identity, status, and allegiance. Paul uses this deeply familiar daily routine of getting dressed to illustrate a profound spiritual shift for the early church in Colossae. The community he addresses was a diverse mix of people from different backgrounds learning how to live together. He urges them to intentionally put on new virtues just as they would put on physical garments each morning. This wasn't about pretending to be perfect, but about adopting a shared uniform that reflected their new identity. The crowning piece of this wardrobe is love, which holds all the other fragile virtues together in community.

## Application for Today

We all have a morning routine before we face the world. Imagine if, alongside choosing our physical clothes, we deliberately decided to wear patience and kindness before leaving the house. Community life is messy, and we will inevitably step on each other's toes. That is exactly why forgiveness and gentleness are presented as daily necessities rather than occasional heroic acts. When we choose to wrap ourselves in love, we create an environment where peace can actually take root and guide our interactions. Today, consider what unseen garments you are putting on, and how they might change the way you treat the people around you.
