## The Reading (NLT)

**John 14:1-7**

> <sup class="verse-num">1</sup> Don't let your hearts be troubled. Trust in God, and trust also in me. <sup class="verse-num">2</sup> There is more than enough room in my Father's home. If this were not so, would I have told you that I am going to prepare a place for you? <sup class="verse-num">3</sup> When everything is ready, I will come and get you, so that you will always be with me where I am. <sup class="verse-num">4</sup> And you know the way to where I am going. <sup class="verse-num">5</sup> No, we don't know, Lord, Thomas said. We have no idea where you are going, so how can we know the way? <sup class="verse-num">6</sup> Jesus told him, I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one can come to the Father except through me. <sup class="verse-num">7</sup> If you had really known me, you would know who my Father is. From now on, you do know him and have seen him!

## The Story

The atmosphere in the upper room is thick with anxiety. Jesus has just upended his disciples' expectations by washing their feet and speaking of imminent betrayal and his own departure. In the midst of this profound uncertainty, he addresses their deepest fears not with a map or a timeline, but with a promise of absolute belonging. He assures them that his leaving is entirely about securing a permanent home for them in the divine presence. When Thomas voices the collective confusion of the group, wanting clear directions, Jesus redirects his focus. The route is not a method or a set of coordinates, but a relationship.

## Application for Today

We often approach our spiritual journey looking for a step-by-step itinerary. When the future feels precarious, our instinct is to demand clarity, asking for the exact route we need to take to find peace. Yet the invitation here is to shift our gaze from the destination to our companion. Trusting in this promise means recognizing that our ultimate security is already being handled by someone who knows us intimately. We do not have to engineer our own belonging or perfectly navigate the unknown. The path forward is simply a relationship, offering us rest and a secure place in the divine presence, even when the road ahead remains obscured.
