## The Reading (NLT)

**Proverbs 3:5-8**

> <sup class="verse-num">5</sup> Trust in the Lord with all your heart; do not depend on your own understanding. <sup class="verse-num">6</sup> Seek his will in all you do, and he will show you which path to take. <sup class="verse-num">7</sup> Don't be impressed with your own wisdom. Instead, fear the Lord and turn away from evil. <sup class="verse-num">8</sup> Then you will have healing for your body and strength for your bones.

## The Story

In ancient Hebrew thought, the heart was not merely the seat of emotion, but the center of intellect, will, and decision-making. Wisdom literature often frames its instruction as a parent guiding a child away from the illusion of self-sufficiency. The writer recognizes a profound human vulnerability: our limited perspective. We naturally want to map out every outcome and rely on our own intellect to navigate life. Yet, the text warns that anchoring our lives solely to our own logic is a fragile foundation. True wisdom begins with reverence for the Divine and a deliberate turning away from arrogance.

## Application for Today

We spend so much energy trying to figure out exactly how our lives will unfold. We analyze, we strategize, and we carry the exhausting burden of trying to control every variable. The invitation here is not to abandon reason, but to surrender our demand for absolute certainty. When we acknowledge God in our daily choices, we shift the heavy lifting off our own shoulders. We find that giving up the need to be the smartest person in the room actually brings a deep, physical sense of relief. Trusting the broader, unseen work of God allows us to walk forward with lighter steps and a quieted mind.
