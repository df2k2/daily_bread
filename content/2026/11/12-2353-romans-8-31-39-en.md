## The Reading (NLT)

**Romans 8:31-39**

> <sup class="verse-num">31</sup> What shall we say about such wonderful things as these? If God is for us, who can ever be against us? <sup class="verse-num">32</sup> Since he did not spare even his own Son but gave him up for us all, won't he also give us everything else? <sup class="verse-num">33</sup> Who dares accuse us whom God has chosen for his own? No one for God himself has given us right standing with himself. <sup class="verse-num">34</sup> Who then will condemn us? Neither death nor life, neither angels nor demons, neither our fears for today nor our worries about tomorrow not even the powers of hell can separate us from God's love. <sup class="verse-num">39</sup> No power in the sky above or in the earth below indeed, nothing in all creation will ever be able to separate us from the love of God that is revealed in Christ Jesus our Lord.

## The Story

Paul writes to a community in Rome that knew the reality of imperial power and the constant threat of sudden persecution. In the first century, the Roman legal system was designed to intimidate, with public accusations and condemnations used to maintain control. Against this backdrop of vulnerability, Paul uses legal and cosmic language to build an airtight case for the security of the believers. He lists extreme hardships like famine, danger, and violence not as hypothetical fears, but as the lived realities of the early church. Yet, he insists that none of these earthly terrors, nor any unseen spiritual forces, hold ultimate jurisdiction over them.

## Application for Today

When we face intense seasons of struggle, it is easy to feel as though we have been abandoned or disqualified. The sheer weight of daily anxieties, sudden crises, or systemic injustices can make the ground beneath us feel incredibly fragile. But this passage offers a profound anchor, reminding us that our standing is not dependent on our circumstances or our ability to hold on perfectly. The love described here is an active, gripping force that holds onto us, even when our own grip fails. No matter what we encounter today, we are tethered to a reality far stronger than our deepest fears, allowing us to move forward with quiet confidence.
