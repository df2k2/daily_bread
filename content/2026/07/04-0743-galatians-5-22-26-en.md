## The Reading (NLT)

**Galatians 5:22-26**

> <sup class="verse-num">22</sup> But the Holy Spirit produces this kind of fruit in our lives: love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, <sup class="verse-num">23</sup> gentleness, and self-control. There is no law against these things! <sup class="verse-num">24</sup> Those who belong to Christ Jesus have nailed the passions and desires of their sinful nature to his cross and crucified them there. <sup class="verse-num">25</sup> Since we are living by the Spirit, let us follow the Spirit's leading in every part of our lives. <sup class="verse-num">26</sup> Let us not become conceited, or provoke one another, or be jealous of one another.

## The Story

When Paul wrote to the early communities in Galatia, they were tangled in fierce debates about rules, behavior, and who truly belonged. They were exhausted by a checklist approach to faith, trying to manage their own impulses through strict regulations. In response, Paul shifts the entire metaphor from a courtroom to an orchard. He describes a natural, organic growth that happens when a person's life is rooted in the divine presence. Instead of manufacturing good behavior through willpower, this transformation is described as a harvest that emerges slowly and naturally over time. The old, self-centered ways of operating are left behind, making room for a completely different way of moving through the world.

## Application for Today

We often treat our spiritual lives like a self-improvement project, keeping score of our successes and failures. But growth does not happen by simply trying harder to be patient or kind. It happens by staying closely connected to the source of life and allowing that presence to shape our reactions. When we let go of our need to compete with others or prove our own worth, we create space for something beautiful to take root. The qualities we desire most deeply are not trophies to be won, but natural outgrowths of a life guided by quiet surrender. Today, rather than forcing yourself to be perfect, focus on simply staying open to the gentle guidance available to you in this very moment.
