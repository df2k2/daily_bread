## The Reading (NLT)

**Psalm 121**

> <sup class="verse-num">1</sup> I look up to the mountainsdoes my help come from there? <sup class="verse-num">2</sup> My help comes from the Lord, who made heaven and earth! <sup class="verse-num">3</sup> He will not let you stumble; the one who watches over you will not slumber. <sup class="verse-num">4</sup> Indeed, he who watches over Israel never slumbers or sleeps. <sup class="verse-num">5</sup> The Lord himself watches over you! The Lord stands beside you as your protective shade. <sup class="verse-num">6</sup> The sun will not harm you by day, nor the moon at night. <sup class="verse-num">7</sup> The Lord keeps you from all harm and watches over your life. <sup class="verse-num">8</sup> The Lord keeps watch over you as you come and go, both now and forever.

## The Story

This ancient poem is known as a Song of Ascents, sung by pilgrims traveling up the treacherous roads to Jerusalem for annual festivals. The journey was physically demanding and fraught with danger from harsh weather, rugged terrain, and bandits. When the traveler looks toward the looming hills, they are not just taking in beautiful scenery; they are assessing real, physical risks. Yet, the song shifts the focus from the intimidating landscape to the one who crafted it. The psalmist reassures the travelers that their ultimate security does not lie in the hills themselves, but in the tireless attention of the Maker.

## Application for Today

We all face stretches of life that feel like an uphill climb, where the obstacles ahead seem overwhelming. It is easy to look at the challenges looming on our horizon and wonder how we will find the strength to navigate them. This ancient song invites us to shift our gaze slightly higher, past the immediate difficulties, to the God who sustains us. We are reminded that we are never walking unattended. Even when we feel exhausted and vulnerable, the one who watches over our steps remains deeply alert, offering a steady, protective presence through every departure and arrival.
