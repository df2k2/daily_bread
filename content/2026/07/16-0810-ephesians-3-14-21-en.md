## The Reading (NLT)

**Ephesians 3:14-21**

> <sup class="verse-num">14</sup> When I think of all this, I fall to my knees and pray to the Father, <sup class="verse-num">15</sup> the Creator of everything in heaven and on earth. <sup class="verse-num">16</sup> I pray that from his glorious, unlimited resources he will empower you with inner strength through his Spirit. <sup class="verse-num">17</sup> Then Christ will make his home in your hearts as you trust in him. Your roots will grow down into God's love and keep you strong. <sup class="verse-num">18</sup> And may you have the power to understand, as all God's people should, how wide, how long, how high, and how deep his love is. <sup class="verse-num">19</sup> May you experience the love of Christ, though it is too great to understand fully. Then you will be made complete with all the fullness of life and power that comes from God. <sup class="verse-num">20</sup> Now all glory to God, who is able, through his mighty power at work within us, to accomplish infinitely more than we might ask or think. <sup class="verse-num">21</sup> Glory to him in the church and in Christ Jesus through all generations forever and ever! Amen.

## The Story

The letter to the Ephesians addresses a diverse, newly formed community learning to live together. In this section, the writer pauses his theological instructions to simply pray for them. His request is not for an easier life or external success, but for a profound internal transformation. He uses agricultural and architectural imagery, asking that they be deeply anchored in a foundation of divine affection. The ultimate hope is that they might grasp the sheer scale of this love, even while acknowledging it defies human comprehension.

## Application for Today

We often try to measure our worth by what we can achieve or how well we hold our lives together. Yet this prayer invites us to stop striving and instead sink our roots into something entirely unearned. When we feel depleted, we are reminded that our inner strength is drawn from an unlimited, eternal wellspring. We do not have to fully figure out how this divine affection works in order to be sustained by it. Trusting in this expansive love gives us the resilience to face whatever comes, knowing we are held by a power far greater than our own imagination.
