## The Reading (NLT)

**Colossians 3:12-17**

> <sup class="verse-num">12</sup> Since God chose you to be the holy people he loves, you must clothe yourselves with tenderhearted mercy, kindness, humility, gentleness, and patience. <sup class="verse-num">13</sup> Make allowance for each other's faults, and forgive anyone who offends you. Remember, the Lord forgave you, so you must forgive others. <sup class="verse-num">14</sup> Above all, clothe yourselves with love, which binds us all together in perfect harmony. <sup class="verse-num">15</sup> And let the peace that comes from Christ rule in your hearts. For as members of one body you are called to live in peace. And always be thankful. <sup class="verse-num">16</sup> Let the message about Christ, in all its richness, fill your lives. Teach and counsel each other with all the wisdom he gives. Sing psalms and hymns and spiritual songs to God with thankful hearts. <sup class="verse-num">17</sup> And whatever you do or say, do it as a representative of the Lord Jesus, giving thanks through him to God the Father.

## The Story

In the ancient Mediterranean world, clothing was a primary marker of identity, status, and allegiance. When the author writes to this early community, he uses the familiar metaphor of getting dressed to describe a profound internal transformation. Instead of putting on garments that signal wealth or power, the followers of this new way are urged to wrap themselves in virtues that build up the community. The text emphasizes that these traits are not just individual achievements but relational tools. They are the very threads that hold a diverse group of people together, creating a resilient fabric woven with intentional grace and mutual forbearance.

## Application for Today

We often treat spiritual growth as a solitary pursuit, a private journey of self-improvement. Yet this passage reminds us that our faith is lived out in the friction and beauty of relationships. When we encounter the inevitable annoyances and deep hurts of living alongside others, we are invited to choose a different response. Allowing a deep, grounding peace to direct our hearts means letting a quiet, steadying presence guide our reactions. It is a daily decision to wear compassion and patience, recognizing that our everyday words and actions have the power to weave a stronger, more beautiful community around us.
