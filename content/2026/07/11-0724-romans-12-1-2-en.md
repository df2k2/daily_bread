## The Reading (NLT)

**Romans 12:1-2**

> <sup class="verse-num">1</sup> And so, dear brothers and sisters, I plead with you to give your bodies to God because of all he has done for you. Let them be a living and holy sacrifice the kind he will find acceptable. This is truly the way to worship him. <sup class="verse-num">2</sup> Don't copy the behavior and customs of this world, but let God transform you into a new person by changing the way you think. Then you will learn to know God's will for you, which is good and pleasing and perfect.

## The Story

In his letter to the Roman church, Paul makes a dramatic pivot from deep theological reflection to practical, everyday living. He borrows familiar language from the temple, where worship meant offering something upon an altar. But instead of a ritual involving a lifeless gift, he proposes something radically different: an ordinary life completely yielded to the divine. In the ancient world, the surrounding culture was driven by imperial power, status, and self-preservation. Paul challenges his readers to resist the prevailing cultural pressures, pointing instead to a profound inner renovation of their thoughts.

## Application for Today

We are constantly being shaped by the world around us. The culture we live in has a gravitational pull, subtly teaching us what to value, what to fear, and how to measure our worth. True devotion interrupts that pull. It is the daily, conscious decision to hand over our ordinary routines, our work, and our relationships as an offering. When we invite the Spirit to reshape our perspectives, we stop reacting to the world's anxieties and start responding with grace. This quiet, internal shift changes our external reality. We begin to recognize the path laid out for us, discovering that this way of living is deeply good and entirely whole.
