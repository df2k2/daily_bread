## The Reading (NLT)

**Lamentations 3:22-26**

> <sup class="verse-num">22</sup> The faithful love of the Lord never ends! His mercies never cease. <sup class="verse-num">23</sup> Great is his faithfulness; his mercies begin afresh each morning. <sup class="verse-num">24</sup> I say to myself, The Lord is my inheritance; therefore, I will hope in him! <sup class="verse-num">25</sup> The Lord is good to those who depend on him, to those who search for him. <sup class="verse-num">26</sup> So it is good to wait quietly for salvation from the Lord.

## The Story

The book of Lamentations was written in the aftermath of unimaginable catastrophe. Jerusalem had fallen, the temple was destroyed, and the survivors were surrounded by the ruins of everything they held dear. The writer is pouring out a song of deep grief, processing trauma and loss in real time. Yet, right in the center of this dark poetry, the tone abruptly shifts. Standing in the ashes of the city, the poet remembers a truth that transcends the current devastation. He realizes that the very fact they are still breathing is evidence of a compassion that has not yet run out.

## Application for Today

It is easy to feel entirely consumed when our circumstances fall apart or when our own failures catch up with us. But the writer offers us a different lens for viewing our survival. God operates on a daily rhythm of grace, resetting with the sunrise regardless of what happened yesterday. We do not have to manufacture our own rescue or panic about the future. Instead, we are invited to sit in the quietness of the present moment and trust that the compassion we need for today has already been provided. We can bring this same steady, renewable grace into our relationships, offering patience to others because we have just received it ourselves.
