## The Reading (NLT)

**Romans 12:1-2**

> <sup class="verse-num">1</sup> And so, dear brothers and sisters, I plead with you to give your bodies to God because of all he has done for you. Let them be a living and holy sacrifice the kind he will find acceptable. This is truly the way to worship him. <sup class="verse-num">2</sup> Don't copy the behavior and customs of this world, but let God transform you into a new person by changing the way you think. Then you will learn to know God's will for you, which is good and pleasing and perfect.

## The Story

Paul was writing to a community living in the very center of the Roman Empire, a culture obsessed with power, status, and self-promotion. In that environment, the pressure to blend in and adopt the empire's values was immense. Animal sacrifices were a daily sight in the capital's temples, representing a transaction to appease angry gods. Paul flips this familiar imagery entirely. Instead of offering something dead on an altar, he invites these early believers to offer their own lives as an ongoing, breathing act of devotion. True worship, he argues, isn't about temple rituals, but about a fundamental shift in how they live and think every single day.

## Application for Today

We are constantly being shaped by the culture around us, often without even realizing it. The world exerts a quiet but relentless pressure to conform to its rhythms of anxiety, consumption, and competition. Resisting this pull doesn't mean retreating from society, but allowing our deepest motivations to be entirely rewired. When we willingly surrender our daily habits and thoughts to God, we begin to see through the shallow promises of our surrounding culture. Our minds become clear enough to recognize what is truly good and life-giving. In that clarity, we find a solid foundation that the shifting pressures of the world cannot shake.
