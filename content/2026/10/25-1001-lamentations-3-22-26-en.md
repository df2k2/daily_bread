## The Reading (NLT)

**Lamentations 3:22-26**

> <sup class="verse-num">22</sup> The faithful love of the Lord never ends! His mercies never cease. <sup class="verse-num">23</sup> Great is his faithfulness; his mercies begin afresh each morning. <sup class="verse-num">24</sup> I say to myself, The Lord is my inheritance; therefore, I will hope in him! <sup class="verse-num">25</sup> The Lord is good to those who depend on him, to those who search for him. <sup class="verse-num">26</sup> So it is good to wait quietly for salvation from the Lord.

## The Story

The book containing this passage is a collection of funeral dirges written after the catastrophic destruction of Jerusalem. The author is surrounded by absolute devastation, witnessing the collapse of everything familiar and secure. Yet, right in the structural center of these agonizing poems, the writer makes a profound pivot. Instead of letting despair have the final word, the narrator remembers the enduring character of God. This realization shifts the focus from the surrounding rubble to the reliable, daily renewal of divine compassion. It is a gritty, hard-won declaration of trust made from the very bottom of a pit.

## Application for Today

We often expect to find hope only when our circumstances improve or our problems are resolved. This passage challenges that instinct by showing that trust can be forged in the middle of our deepest grief. When life feels shattered, we are invited to look for the small, daily arrivals of grace rather than demanding immediate rescue. Waiting in silence is not a passive surrender to despair, but an active, anchoring choice to rely on divine goodness. We can face today knowing that the compassion we need has already been restocked for us. Even in our darkest moments, we are held by a love that refuses to run dry.
