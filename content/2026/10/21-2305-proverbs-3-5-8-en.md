## The Reading (NLT)

**Proverbs 3:5-8**

> <sup class="verse-num">5</sup> Trust in the Lord with all your heart; do not depend on your own understanding. <sup class="verse-num">6</sup> Seek his will in all you do, and he will show you which path to take. <sup class="verse-num">7</sup> Don't be impressed with your own wisdom. Instead, fear the Lord and turn away from evil. <sup class="verse-num">8</sup> Then you will have healing for your body and strength for your bones.

## The Story

The opening chapters of this ancient collection of wisdom are framed as a deeply personal conversation between a parent and a child. The teacher knows that human nature constantly drives us to figure everything out on our own, building our lives on the shaky ground of our limited perspective. In the ancient Near East, the heart was considered the center of thought and will, not just emotion. To give that over completely meant surrendering the illusion of control. The writer contrasts the exhaustion of self-reliance with the vitality that comes from recognizing our place before the Creator.

## Application for Today

We spend so much energy trying to map out every detail of our futures, analyzing every possible outcome until we are paralyzed by anxiety. This passage offers a profound release from that burden, suggesting that true direction comes not from having all the answers, but from leaning into the One who sees the whole picture. When we stop demanding to understand every twist in the road, we make room to simply walk with God in the present moment. Releasing our tight grip on our own cleverness does not make us naive; it makes us free. In that surrender, we often find a deep sense of relief, as the heavy weight of self-sufficiency is finally lifted from our shoulders.
