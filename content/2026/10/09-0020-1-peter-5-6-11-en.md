## The Reading (NLT)

**1 Peter 5:6-11**

> <sup class="verse-num">6</sup> So humble yourselves under the mighty power of God, and at the right time he will lift you up in honor. <sup class="verse-num">7</sup> Give all your worries and cares to God, for he cares about you. <sup class="verse-num">8</sup> Stay alert! Watch out for your great enemy, the devil. He prowls around like a roaring lion, looking for someone to devour. <sup class="verse-num">9</sup> Stand firm against him, and be strong in your faith. Remember that your family of believers all over the world is going through the same kind of suffering you are. <sup class="verse-num">10</sup> In his kindness God called you to share in his eternal glory by means of Christ Jesus. So after you have suffered a little while, he will restore, support, and strengthen you, and he will place you on a firm foundation. <sup class="verse-num">11</sup> All power to him forever! Amen.

## The Story

The early Christians scattered across the ancient world often felt isolated and vulnerable. They faced intense social pressure and the constant threat of persecution, creating an atmosphere of deep anxiety. The author of this letter captures their dread with the imagery of a prowling predator looking for easy prey. Yet, the letter shifts the focus from fear to a profound sense of solidarity, reminding these scattered communities that their struggles are shared by believers everywhere. Instead of relying on their own strength, they are invited to find security under the protective care of a God who sees their burdens.

## Application for Today

Anxiety has a way of making us feel completely alone in our struggles. When worries begin to consume our thoughts, it is easy to believe that we are the only ones facing such heavy burdens. This passage invites us to release that crushing weight, trusting that we are held by a God who is deeply invested in our well-being. It also calls us to stay awake to the destructive habits or fears that threaten to pull us under. By remembering that suffering is a shared human experience, we can find the courage to stand firm, trusting that a season of hardship will eventually give way to a restored and stable foundation.
