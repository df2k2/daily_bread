## The Reading (NLT)

**Proverbs 3:5-8**

Instead, fear the Lord and turn away from evil. <sup class="verse-num">8</sup> Then you will have healing for your body and strength for your bones.

## The Story

The ancient wisdom tradition frequently contrasts two ways of moving through the world: relying on our own limited perspective or yielding to a broader, divine vision. In the ancient Near East, the heart was considered the center of human intellect and decision-making, not merely the seat of emotion. Therefore, committing the whole heart to God meant surrendering one's intellect and will, acknowledging that human comprehension has boundaries. The writer warns against the intoxicating illusion of self-sufficiency, framing true wisdom as a humble reverence that actively steps away from arrogance. This posture of deep reliance was understood to bring profound stability, affecting even the physical vitality of a person.

## Application for Today

We live in an era that prizes having all the answers and optimizing every outcome. It is exhausting to constantly carry the burden of figuring everything out, especially when life becomes unpredictable or overwhelming. True peace begins when we admit the limits of our own logic and stop trying to micromanage our futures. Yielding our plans to a higher wisdom does not mean abandoning our intellect, but rather refusing to let it be the ultimate authority. When we release the desperate need to control every variable, we often find a deep, quiet relief that restores our weary minds and bodies. Trust becomes less about knowing the exact destination and more about walking confidently with the guide.
