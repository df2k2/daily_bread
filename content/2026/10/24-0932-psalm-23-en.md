## The Reading (NLT)

**Psalm 23**

> <sup class="verse-num">1</sup> The Lord is my shepherd; I have all that I need. <sup class="verse-num">2</sup> He lets me rest in green meadows; he leads me beside peaceful streams. <sup class="verse-num">3</sup> He renews my strength. He guides me along right paths, bringing honor to his name. <sup class="verse-num">4</sup> Even when I walk through the darkest valley, I will not be afraid, for you are close beside me. Your rod and your staff protect and comfort me. <sup class="verse-num">5</sup> You prepare a feast for me in the presence of my enemies. You honor me by anointing my head with oil. My cup overflows with blessings. <sup class="verse-num">6</sup> Surely your goodness and unfailing love will pursue me all the days of my life, and I will live in the house of the Lord forever.

## The Story

In the ancient Near East, tending a flock was a rugged, hands-on task requiring constant vigilance to secure safe grazing and water. Midway through this ancient song, the imagery shifts from a rural landscape to the intimacy of a host welcoming a traveler. In nomadic cultures, offering a meal and anointing a visitor were profound acts of covenant hospitality. Once someone was received into the tent, the host was bound by honor to defend them against any outside threat. The writer weaves these metaphors together to describe a Divine presence that both navigates treacherous terrain and offers fierce, welcoming protection.

## Application for Today

We often wish our lives were entirely free of looming threats or difficult transitions. Yet this passage does not promise the absence of shadows or adversaries. Instead, it anchors our hope in the proximity of a guide who knows the way forward. When exhaustion sets in or circumstances feel overwhelming, you do not have to manufacture your own security. There is a space of welcome already prepared for you, a place of quiet restoration right in the middle of your struggles. You can pause, trusting that you are deeply cared for and that relentless grace is following close behind.
