## The Reading (NLT)

**2 Corinthians 4:16-18**

> <sup class="verse-num">16</sup> That is why we never give up. Though our bodies are dying, our spirits are being renewed every day. <sup class="verse-num">17</sup> For our present troubles are small and won't last very long. Yet they produce for us a glory that vastly outweighs them and will last forever! <sup class="verse-num">18</sup> So we don't look at the troubles we can see now; rather, we fix our gaze on things that cannot be seen. For the things we see now will soon be gone, but the things we cannot see will last forever.

## The Story

Paul was writing to a community in Corinth that valued status, physical impressiveness, and visible success. In contrast, his own life was marked by shipwrecks, beatings, and constant exhaustion. He acknowledged the undeniable reality of physical decline and the heavy toll that life takes on the human body. Yet, he framed this outward wearing away as a stark contrast to a hidden, vibrant reality happening inside. The apostle was inviting his friends to recognize a deeper current of life that remains untouched by physical decay or temporary hardships.

## Application for Today

It is incredibly easy to be consumed by what is right in front of us, especially when it involves pain, aging, or profound disappointment. The struggles we face are very real and can feel overwhelmingly heavy in the moment. However, this passage invites us to shift our focus just slightly, looking past the immediate horizon to something far more enduring. True renewal often happens quietly, in the hidden spaces of our hearts, even when our circumstances remain chaotic. By anchoring our attention to the eternal, we find the quiet strength to keep moving forward, trusting that our current difficulties are not the final word.
