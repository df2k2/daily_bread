## The Reading (NLT)

**Isaiah 40:28-31**

> <sup class="verse-num">28</sup> Have you never heard? Have you never understood? The Lord is the everlasting God, the Creator of all the earth. He never grows weak or weary. No one can measure the depths of his understanding. <sup class="verse-num">29</sup> He gives power to the weak and strength to the powerless. <sup class="verse-num">30</sup> Even youths will become weak and tired, and young men will fall in exhaustion. <sup class="verse-num">31</sup> But those who trust in the Lord will find new strength. They will soar high on wings like eagles. They will run and not grow weary. They will walk and not faint.

## The Story

The exiled Israelites were living through a period of profound disillusionment, convinced that their Creator had either forgotten them or was simply unable to rescue them from Babylon. In response, the prophet shifts their gaze away from their immediate political reality and toward the vastness of the cosmos. He reminds a defeated people that the architect of the universe is not bound by human limitations, fatigue, or the passage of time. The text contrasts the inevitable exhaustion of even the most vigorous young warriors with the limitless vitality of the Divine. It suggests that human endurance has a hard ceiling, but divine energy is an inexhaustible well. The promise given to the exiles is not an immediate escape from their long journey, but a profound exchange of their failing energy for an enduring stamina.

## Application for Today

We often operate under the illusion that if we just try harder, sleep a little better, or manage our time more efficiently, we can overcome any obstacle on our own. Yet, this ancient poetry gently confronts us with the reality that human capacity will always eventually run dry. When we hit the wall of our own limitations, it is not a sign of failure, but an invitation to rely on a source of power outside ourselves. Waiting on the Divine requires us to stop frantic striving and acknowledge our deep need. In that quiet posture of dependence, we are given the resilience to keep moving forward. Whether the road ahead requires us to rise above a sudden crisis or simply put one foot in front of the other on a mundane day, the strength provided is exactly what the moment demands.
