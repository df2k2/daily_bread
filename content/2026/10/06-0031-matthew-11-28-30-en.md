## The Reading (NLT)

**Matthew 11:28-30**

> <sup class="verse-num">28</sup> Then Jesus said, Come to me, all of you who are weary and carry heavy burdens, and I will give you rest. <sup class="verse-num">29</sup> Take my yoke upon you. Let me teach you, because I am humble and gentle at heart, and you will find rest for your souls. <sup class="verse-num">30</sup> For my yoke is easy to bear, and the burden I give you is light.

## The Story

In first-century agrarian societies, a yoke was a familiar farming tool used to pair oxen together for plowing fields. Often, an older, experienced animal would be yoked alongside a younger one to guide the pace and bear the majority of the weight. The religious teachers of the time were known to place heavy, complex legalistic demands on the people, creating an exhausting system of rules that felt impossible to satisfy. Jesus contrasts this exhausting religious framework with his own approach to life and faith. He invites his listeners into a shared harness, stepping into the heavy lifting alongside them. Instead of adding more demands, he offers a relationship defined by compassion and shared effort.

## Application for Today

We often treat faith as a relentless checklist of moral achievements or a ladder we have to climb entirely on our own strength. When we are exhausted by the demands of our careers, families, and even our own internal expectations, the message here is to stop pulling the plow alone. Stepping into the way of Jesus does not mean all our responsibilities suddenly vanish. Instead, it means we are no longer bearing the crushing weight of life in isolation. We are invited to match our pace to a guide whose leadership is marked by deep compassion rather than rigid expectations. True rest is found not in abandoning our work, but in sharing the load with one who knows our limits and offers grace.
