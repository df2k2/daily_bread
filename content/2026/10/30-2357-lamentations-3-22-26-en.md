## The Reading (NLT)

**Lamentations 3:22-26**

> <sup class="verse-num">22</sup> The faithful love of the Lord never ends! His mercies never cease. <sup class="verse-num">23</sup> Great is his faithfulness; his mercies begin afresh each morning. <sup class="verse-num">24</sup> I say to myself, The Lord is my inheritance; therefore, I will hope in him! <sup class="verse-num">25</sup> The Lord is good to those who depend on him, to those who search for him. <sup class="verse-num">26</sup> So it is good to wait quietly for salvation from the Lord.

## The Story

The book of Lamentations was written in the aftermath of a catastrophic national trauma. Jerusalem lay in ruins, the temple was destroyed, and the people were facing exile and starvation. The poet is walking through a landscape of profound grief, surrounded by the physical and emotional rubble of a shattered world. Yet, right in the center of this funeral dirge, the writer makes a stunning pivot. Surrounded by endings, the poet remembers that the compassion of the Creator has no expiration date. The focus shifts from the overwhelming devastation to the steady, rhythmic arrival of dawn, bringing a reminder of divine reliability.

## Application for Today

When we are overwhelmed by the wreckage of our own plans or the deep uncertainties of life, our minds often spiral into despair. We try to carry the weight of everything we have lost alongside everything we fear is coming. But this passage invites us to narrow our focus to the span of a single day. We do not have to figure out how to survive the distant future; we only need to look for the grace provided for today. Just as the sun rises without our effort, divine compassion meets us exactly where we are, offering enough strength for the present moment. Waiting quietly is not about passive resignation, but an active, gentle trust that we are held by a love that outlasts our darkest nights.
