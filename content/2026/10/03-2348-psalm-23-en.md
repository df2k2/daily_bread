## The Reading (NLT)

**Psalm 23**

> <sup class="verse-num">1</sup> The Lord is my shepherd; I have all that I need. <sup class="verse-num">2</sup> He lets me rest in green meadows; he leads me beside peaceful streams. <sup class="verse-num">3</sup> He renews my strength. He guides me along right paths, bringing honor to his name. <sup class="verse-num">4</sup> Even when I walk through the darkest valley, I will not be afraid, for you are close beside me. Your rod and your staff protect and comfort me. <sup class="verse-num">5</sup> You prepare a feast for me in the presence of my enemies. You honor me by anointing my head with oil. My cup overflows with blessings. <sup class="verse-num">6</sup> Surely your goodness and unfailing love will pursue me all the days of my life, and I will live in the house of the Lord forever.

## The Story

In the ancient Near East, a shepherd's job was both intimate and dangerous. Flocks required constant movement to find fresh grazing land and water sources, often leading them through steep, shadowed ravines. These deep valleys were fraught with real threats, from predators hidden in the rocks to sudden flash floods. Yet, the presence of the shepherd changed the entire experience for the vulnerable flock. The song shifts its imagery from a pasture to a royal banquet, reflecting ancient customs of hospitality. In a culture where a host was entirely responsible for a guest's safety, being welcomed to the table meant absolute security. The journey through hazardous terrain ultimately ends in a place of radical, abundant welcome.

## Application for Today

We often expect that a faithful life will somehow bypass the dark and difficult valleys. However, this ancient song does not promise the absence of danger, but rather the absolute guarantee of divine companionship. When the terrain of your life becomes frightening or exhausting, you are not wandering alone. The same care that guides you to moments of rest also walks closely beside you in seasons of deep uncertainty. You are being led toward a place where you are fiercely protected and deeply honored. Even when surrounded by threats, you can find a quiet, defiant peace at a table prepared just for you. Rest in the knowledge that goodness and love are actively pursuing you today.
