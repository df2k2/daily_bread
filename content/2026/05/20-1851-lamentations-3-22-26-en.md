## The Reading (NLT)

**Lamentations 3:22-26**

> The faithful love of the Lord never ends! His mercies never cease. Great is his faithfulness; his mercies begin afresh each morning. I say to myself, The Lord is my inheritance; therefore, I will hope in him! The Lord is good to those who depend on him, to those who search for him. So it is good to wait quietly for salvation from the Lord.

## The Story

The book of Lamentations was written in the immediate aftermath of a catastrophic national trauma. Jerusalem had been sacked, its temple destroyed, and its people carried off into exile. The poet spends chapters pouring out raw, unfiltered grief over the devastation of everything familiar. Yet, right in the structural center of this collection of dirges, the tone suddenly shifts. Surrounded by the literal and figurative rubble of his world, the writer makes a deliberate choice to remember the enduring character of his Creator.

## Application for Today

It is profoundly difficult to look for light when everything around us feels like it is collapsing. We often assume that faith requires us to ignore our pain or pretend the ruins are not real. But this ancient poet shows us a different way forward, acknowledging the heartbreak while anchoring his mind to a deeper reality. Choosing to sit in stillness is not passive resignation, but an active, courageous stance of trust. When we wake up to face another difficult day, the morning itself becomes a testament to a love that outlasts our darkest nights.
