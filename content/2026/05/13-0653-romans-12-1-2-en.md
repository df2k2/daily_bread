## The Reading (NLT)

**Romans 12:1-2**

> <sup class="verse-num">1</sup> And so, dear brothers and sisters, I plead with you to give your bodies to God because of all he has done for you. Let them be a living and holy sacrifice the kind he will find acceptable. This is truly the way to worship him. <sup class="verse-num">2</sup> Don't copy the behavior and customs of this world, but let God transform you into a new person by changing the way you think. Then you will learn to know God's will for you, which is good and pleasing and perfect.

## The Story

Paul writes these words as a major turning point in his letter to the early church in Rome. After spending chapters explaining the profound mercy and grace of God, he shifts to what that means for everyday life. He uses the familiar religious imagery of temple sacrifices but turns it completely upside down. Instead of bringing an animal to an altar to be killed, believers are asked to bring their own lives, fully alive and active. This act of offering one's entire self is described as the most logical and authentic form of worship. It moves faith out of a building and into the reality of daily existence.

## Application for Today

It is incredibly easy to let the surrounding culture dictate our values, habits, and desires. We are constantly pressured to fit into molds defined by success, outrage, or consumption. Yet, the invitation here is to resist that external pressure by allowing an internal transformation to take place. When we open our minds to be renewed by God's grace, our perspective begins to shift. We start to see our time, energy, and bodies not as tools for our own advancement, but as instruments of love. In doing so, we discover a way of living that is deeply aligned with what is good and life-giving.
