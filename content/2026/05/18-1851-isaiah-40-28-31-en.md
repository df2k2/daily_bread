## The Reading (NLT)

**Isaiah 40:28-31**

> Have you never heard? Have you never understood? The Lord is the everlasting God, the Creator of all the earth. He never grows weak or weary. No one can measure the depths of his understanding. He gives power to the weak and strength to the powerless. Even youths will become weak and tired, and young men will fall in exhaustion. But those who trust in the Lord will find new strength. They will soar high on wings like eagles. They will run and not grow weary. They will walk and not faint.

## The Story

The ancient Israelites were living in exile far from home and feeling entirely abandoned by their Creator. They looked around at their ruined circumstances and assumed the Divine had either forgotten them or lacked the power to save them. The prophet addresses this deep communal fatigue by reminding them of the nature of God. Unlike human empires that rise and inevitably collapse from exhaustion, the Creator exists outside the bounds of finite energy. The text contrasts the absolute peak of human vitality with limitless divine stamina. It suggests that even the most vibrant and capable among us will eventually hit a wall.

## Application for Today

We live in a culture that prizes constant production and endless hustle, often pushing us past our physical and emotional limits. When we inevitably crash, we tend to feel a profound sense of failure. This passage offers a gentle corrective, normalizing our human exhaustion while pointing us toward a source of renewal that does not depend on our own grit. Trusting in the Divine is not a guarantee of an easy life, but an invitation to plug into an enduring and quiet strength. It is a reminder that we do not have to manufacture our own momentum every single day. We are allowed to rest, recalibrate, and rely on a power far greater than our own fatigue.
