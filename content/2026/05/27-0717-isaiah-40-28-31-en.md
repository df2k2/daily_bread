## The Reading (NLT)

**Isaiah 40:28-31**

> <sup class="verse-num">28</sup> Have you never heard? Have you never understood? The Lord is the everlasting God, the Creator of all the earth. He never grows weak or weary. No one can measure the depths of his understanding. <sup class="verse-num">29</sup> He gives power to the weak and strength to the powerless. <sup class="verse-num">30</sup> Even youths will become weak and tired, and young men will fall in exhaustion. <sup class="verse-num">31</sup> But those who trust in the Lord will find new strength. They will soar high on wings like eagles. They will run and not grow weary. They will walk and not faint.

## The Story

The prophet addresses a community in deep despair, likely wrestling with the trauma of exile and the feeling that their divine protector has forgotten them. They are spiritually and physically depleted, looking around at the ruins of their former lives. In response, the text points them away from their immediate circumstances and toward the vast, untiring nature of the Creator. It acknowledges a hard truth of the human condition: no matter how resilient or capable we are, our natural reserves eventually run dry. Even the most vibrant among us will inevitably hit a wall. Yet, the promise offered is not a sudden removal of the journey, but a profound exchange of our finite energy for an infinite supply.

## Application for Today

We often try to navigate our darkest moments using only our own willpower, treating our faith like a test of endurance. We push forward until we are entirely hollowed out, afraid that admitting our exhaustion means we have failed. But this passage invites us into a place of vulnerability, showing us that divine support begins exactly where our self-reliance ends. When we stop trying to manufacture our own momentum, we create space to receive a deeper, sustaining grace. We are not asked to be tireless; we are asked to lean into the One who is. In that quiet surrender, we discover a steady wind beneath us, carrying us through the miles we could never walk alone.
