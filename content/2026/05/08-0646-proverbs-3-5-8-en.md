## The Reading (NLT)

**Proverbs 3:5-8**

> <sup class="verse-num">5</sup> Trust in the Lord with all your heart; do not depend on your own understanding. <sup class="verse-num">6</sup> Seek his will in all you do, and he will show you which path to take. <sup class="verse-num">7</sup> Don't be impressed with your own wisdom. Instead, fear the Lord and turn away from evil. <sup class="verse-num">8</sup> Then you will have healing for your body and strength for your bones.

## The Story

Ancient wisdom literature often centers on the tension between human intellect and divine guidance. In the ancient Near East, wisdom was highly prized as the key to a successful life, and teachers passed down practical advice to the next generation. However, this particular instruction introduces a radical shift in perspective. It suggests that human intellect, while valuable, is inherently limited and flawed when isolated from the Creator. True wisdom begins with a profound humility, recognizing that our vantage point is restricted. The ancient writer emphasizes that releasing our grip on self-reliance opens the door to a deeper, more profound direction.

## Application for Today

We live in a culture that rewards having all the answers and planning every step of our lives. When faced with uncertainty, our instinct is usually to analyze our way out of the problem. Yet, this ancient guidance invites us to surrender our exhausting need for total control. Acknowledging a higher guidance does not mean abandoning our minds, but rather aligning our intellect with a broader, sacred reality. When we stop trying to outsmart our circumstances, we often find a surprising sense of physical and emotional relief. Trusting a greater path brings a deep, grounding peace to our exhausted bodies and minds.
