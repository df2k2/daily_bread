## The Reading (NLT)

**John 3:16-21**

> For this is how God loved the world: He gave his one and only Son, so that everyone who believes in him will not perish but have eternal life. God sent his Son into the world not to judge the world, but to save the world through him. There is no judgment against anyone who believes in him. But anyone who does not believe in him has already been judged for not believing in God's one and only Son. And the judgment is based on this fact: God's light came into the world, but people loved the darkness more than the light, for their actions were evil. All who do evil hate the light and refuse to go near it for fear their sins will be exposed. But those who do what is right come to the light so others can see that they are doing what God wants.

## The Story

The Gospel of John often uses bold contrasts to illustrate spiritual realities, and here the imagery of light and darkness takes center stage. This passage follows a nighttime conversation with a religious leader, making the metaphor of stepping into the light especially poignant. In the first-century Jewish context, light was a well-known symbol for truth and divine presence. The writer emphasizes that the heavenly rescue mission was driven by profound affection for the entire world, rather than a desire to condemn it. The tragedy highlighted is not that humanity is inherently unlovable, but that people often actively hide from this rescuing light. They retreat into the shadows to conceal their brokenness.

## Application for Today

It is a common human instinct to hide our flaws, fearing that if we are truly seen, we will be rejected. Yet this text invites us to reframe how we view divine exposure. The light is not a harsh spotlight meant to shame us, but a healing dawn designed to bring us freedom. When we stop hiding and allow our lives to be illuminated, we find that the initial discomfort of vulnerability gives way to profound peace. We are invited to bring our whole selves into the open, trusting that the motivation behind this light is unimaginable love. Living in the truth means embracing the reality that we are known completely and cherished eternally.
