## The Reading (NLT)

**Romans 12:1-2**

> And so, dear brothers and sisters, I plead with you to give your bodies to God because of all he has done for you. Let them be a living and holy sacrifice the kind he will find acceptable. This is truly the way to worship him. Don't copy the behavior and customs of this world, but let God transform you into a new person by changing the way you think. Then you will learn to know God's will for you, which is good and pleasing and perfect.

## The Story

In the ancient world, worship was almost universally understood as offering something dead on an altar. Paul flips this deeply ingrained cultural script on its head by asking for a sacrifice that continues to breathe. After spending eleven chapters outlining the vast mercy of God, he shifts his focus to our response. He borrows the language of the temple but moves the location of worship from a stone building to the human body and mind. The call is not to abandon the physical world, but to inhabit it differently. Instead of being pressed into the mold of the surrounding culture, believers are invited into an ongoing process of inner renovation.

## Application for Today

We face constant pressure to conform to the values, anxieties, and rhythms of the society around us. This cultural current is strong, and drifting along with it requires no effort at all. True transformation, however, begins from the inside out, starting with how we think and what we value. When we intentionally offer our daily habits, thoughts, and bodies to God, we interrupt the default programming of the world. This is how we begin to recognize a different path forward. As our minds are reshaped by grace, we find that divine desires for our lives become clearer, bringing a deep sense of peace and purpose.
