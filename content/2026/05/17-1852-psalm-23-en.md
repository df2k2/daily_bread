## The Reading (NLT)

**Psalm 23**

> <sup class="verse-num">1</sup> The Lord is my shepherd; I have all that I need. <sup class="verse-num">2</sup> He lets me rest in green meadows; he leads me beside peaceful streams. <sup class="verse-num">3</sup> He renews my strength. He guides me along right paths, bringing honor to his name. <sup class="verse-num">4</sup> Even when I walk through the darkest valley, I will not be afraid, for you are close beside me. Your rod and your staff protect and comfort me. <sup class="verse-num">5</sup> You prepare a feast for me in the presence of my enemies. You honor me by anointing my head with oil. My cup overflows with blessings. <sup class="verse-num">6</sup> Surely your goodness and unfailing love will pursue me all the days of my life, and I will live in the house of the Lord forever.

## The Story

In ancient Near Eastern culture, the relationship between a shepherd and their flock was one of intimate, constant care. The terrain of Judea was rugged and dangerous, filled with steep ravines, arid deserts, and sudden predators. A good caretaker had to intimately know where to find safe water and hidden patches of grass to sustain the animals. Furthermore, this role transitioned into that of a gracious host when travelers sought refuge. Offering a lavish meal and anointing a guest with fragrant oil were powerful cultural symbols of absolute protection and abundant provision. The host took personal responsibility for the safety of anyone under their roof, even if adversaries waited just outside the door.

## Application for Today

We often want our spiritual journey to be a continuous stretch of easy, sunlit meadows. Yet, this passage acknowledges that deep valleys and looming threats are inevitable parts of the human experience. The promise offered here is not the absence of danger, but the presence of a faithful guide who walks closely beside us. When anxiety or opposition surrounds you, you do not have to scramble for your own defense or sustenance. There is a quiet, steady provision available right in the middle of the hardship. You can sit down, rest, and receive nourishment even while the storm rages outside. True security comes from knowing who is hosting you at the table.
