## The Reading (NLT)

**Isaiah 40:28-31**

> Have you never heard? Have you never understood? The Lord is the everlasting God, the Creator of all the earth. He never grows weak or weary. No one can measure the depths of his understanding. He gives power to the weak and strength to the powerless. Even youths will become weak and tired, and young men will fall in exhaustion. But those who trust in the Lord will find new strength. They will soar high on wings like eagles. They will run and not grow weary. They will walk and not faint.

## The Story

The ancient Israelites receiving this message were living in exile, displaced from their home and feeling entirely forgotten. Decades of captivity had drained their collective spirit, leaving them questioning if their Creator still cared or had the power to intervene. The prophet addresses this deep communal exhaustion by shifting their focus away from their immediate political reality and toward the eternal nature of the Divine. He reminds them that human vitality always has a breaking point, regardless of age or physical condition. Yet, he offers a profound reassurance that divine energy is inexhaustible and generously shared with those who are completely depleted.

## Application for Today

We often live under the illusion that we need to be endlessly productive, strong, and self-sufficient. When we inevitably hit a wall, we feel a deep sense of failure or isolation, assuming we are meant to carry our burdens alone. This passage invites us to release the pressure of constant self-reliance and lean into a strength that is far greater than our own. Waiting on the Divine is not a passive, helpless state, but an active posture of trust that opens us up to genuine renewal. When we admit our own limitations, we create space for grace to sustain us through long seasons of difficulty. Whether we are facing a momentary challenge or a prolonged marathon, we are held by an enduring presence that never tires.
