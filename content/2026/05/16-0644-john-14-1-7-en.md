## The Reading (NLT)

**John 14:1-7**

> Don't let your hearts be troubled. Trust in God, and trust also in me. There is more than enough room in my Father's home. If this were not so, would I have told you that I am going to prepare a place for you? When everything is ready, I will come and get you, so that you will always be with me where I am. And you know the way to where I am going. No, we don't know, Lord, Thomas said. We have no idea where you are going, so how can we know the way? Jesus told him, I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one can come to the Father except through me. If you had really known me, you would know who my Father is. From now on, you do know him and have seen him!

## The Story

The atmosphere in the upper room is heavy with dread. Jesus has just spoken about betrayal, his imminent departure, and the shattering of their tight-knit group. The disciples are facing the collapse of everything they thought they understood about their future. In the midst of this profound disorientation, Jesus speaks directly to their anxiety rather than dismissing it. He shifts their focus from the impending crisis to a broader reality of divine hospitality. Thomas, voicing the collective confusion, admits they are completely lost. Rather than handing them a map, the response given centers their hope entirely on relationship and an enduring connection with the Creator.

## Application for Today

It is entirely human to feel overwhelmed when our anticipated future suddenly dissolves. Like the early disciples, we often demand clear directions and certainty before we are willing to take the next step. Yet the comfort offered here is not a detailed itinerary of what comes next. It is an invitation to trust a presence over a plan. When the road ahead is obscured by grief or transition, we do not need to have all the answers figured out. Our peace is found in the assurance that we are deeply known, securely held, and ultimately headed toward a spacious welcome.
