## The Reading (NLT)

**Matthew 11:28-30**

> <sup class="verse-num">28</sup> Then Jesus said, Come to me, all of you who are weary and carry heavy burdens, and I will give you rest. <sup class="verse-num">29</sup> Take my yoke upon you. Let me teach you, because I am humble and gentle at heart, and you will find rest for your souls. <sup class="verse-num">30</sup> For my yoke is easy to bear, and the burden I give you is light.

## The Story

In the first century, the people listening to this invitation were exhausted. They lived under the crushing taxation and military presence of the Roman Empire, while also navigating complex, rigid religious laws that demanded constant striving. When religious teachers spoke of a yoke, they were usually referring to the heavy obligations of the law, a metaphor drawn from the wooden harnesses used to hitch oxen together for labor. Jesus steps into this cultural exhaustion with a radical alternative. He does not promise a life without work or responsibility, but he offers a completely different way to carry the weight. His apprenticeship is marked by gentleness, not harsh demands.

## Application for Today

We still know what it means to be bone-tired today. We carry the weight of our own expectations, the relentless pace of modern life, and the quiet anxieties that keep us awake at night. The invitation here is not to abandon all our responsibilities, but to change who we are hitched to. When we try to pull the weight of our lives entirely on our own, we quickly burn out. Walking alongside a humble teacher changes the nature of the effort. We are invited to trade our frantic striving for a shared, steady pace, discovering that true relief comes from learning to move through life in step with grace.
