## The Reading (NLT)

**1 Corinthians 13**

> <sup class="verse-num">1</sup> If I could speak all the languages of earth and of angels, but didn't love others, I would only be a noisy gong or a clanging cymbal. <sup class="verse-num">2</sup> If I had the gift of prophecy, and if I understood all of God's secret plans and possessed all knowledge, and if I had such faith that I could move mountains, but didn't love others, I would be nothing. <sup class="verse-num">3</sup> If I gave everything I have to the poor and even sacrificed my body, I could boast about it; but if I didn't love others, I would have gained nothing. <sup class="verse-num">4</sup> Love is patient and kind. It is not irritable, and it keeps no record of being wronged. <sup class="verse-num">6</sup> It does not rejoice about injustice but rejoices whenever the truth wins out. <sup class="verse-num">7</sup> Love never gives up, never loses faith, is always hopeful, and endures through every circumstance. <sup class="verse-num">8</sup> Prophecy and speaking in unknown languages and special knowledge will become useless. But love will last forever! <sup class="verse-num">9</sup> Now our knowledge is partial and incomplete, and even the gift of prophecy reveals only part of the whole picture! <sup class="verse-num">10</sup> But when the time of perfection comes, these partial things will become useless. <sup class="verse-num">11</sup> When I was a child, I spoke and thought and reasoned as a child. All that I know now is partial and incomplete, but then I will know everything completely, just as God now knows me completely. <sup class="verse-num">13</sup> Three things will last forever faith, hope, and love and the greatest of these is love.

## The Story

Paul was writing to a community obsessed with status and spiritual achievements. The church in Corinth valued flashy displays of faith, profound knowledge, and dramatic acts of sacrifice. They were constantly measuring themselves against one another, trying to prove who was the most spiritual. Paul interrupts their striving to offer a completely different metric for a life well-lived. He dismantles the idea that impressive abilities or even extreme generosity matter if the core motivation is empty. For Paul, the foundation of the community had to be something far more enduring than individual talent or temporary spiritual highs.

## Application for Today

It is incredibly easy to get caught up in doing the right things for the wrong reasons. We can build impressive resumes, volunteer our time, and cultivate deep knowledge, yet still miss the point entirely. This passage invites us to examine the quiet engine driving our actions. True love is not a fleeting emotion; it is a steady, patient, and humble way of moving through the world. It requires us to lay down our need to be right, to be noticed, or to be in control. When everything else fades away, the only thing that will remain is how well we chose to love the people around us.
