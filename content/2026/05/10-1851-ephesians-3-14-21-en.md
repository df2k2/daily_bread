## The Reading (NLT)

**Ephesians 3:14-21**

> When I think of all this, I fall to my knees and pray to the Father, the Creator of everything in heaven and on earth. I pray that from his glorious, unlimited resources he will empower you with inner strength through his Spirit. Then Christ will make his home in your hearts as you trust in him. Your roots will grow down into God's love and keep you strong. And may you have the power to understand, as all God's people should, how wide, how long, how high, and how deep his love is. May you experience the love of Christ, though it is too great to understand fully. Then you will be made complete with all the fullness of life and power that comes from God. Now all glory to God, who is able, through his mighty power at work within us, to accomplish infinitely more than we might ask or think. Glory to him in the church and in Christ Jesus through all generations forever and ever! Amen.

## The Story

Paul wrote this letter to a community living in a culturally diverse and often overwhelming ancient city. He wanted them to grasp the sheer scale of the divine affection directed toward them. Instead of offering a checklist of behaviors, he focuses on their internal foundation. He uses the imagery of deep roots and solid architecture to describe how a community is built on something entirely unshakeable. The goal was never just intellectual agreement, but a lived experience of a reality that defies human measurement.

## Application for Today

We often measure our spiritual lives by what we accomplish or how well we hold things together. Yet this passage invites us to measure our lives by the dimensions of divine love. When we feel depleted, we are reminded that our internal strength draws from an infinite well, not our own limited reserves. We do not have to manufacture this stability; we only need to sink our roots into it. By trusting in that expansive affection, we find the capacity to face our daily lives with an unexpected and quiet resilience.
