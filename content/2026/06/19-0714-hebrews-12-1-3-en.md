## The Reading (NLT)

**Hebrews 12:1-3**

> <sup class="verse-num">1</sup> Therefore, since we are surrounded by such a huge crowd of witnesses to the life of faith, let us strip off every weight that slows us down, especially the sin that so easily trips us up. And let us run with endurance the race God has set before us. <sup class="verse-num">2</sup> We do this by keeping our eyes on Jesus, the champion who initiates and perfects our faith. Because of the joy awaiting him, he endured the cross, disregarding its shame. Now he is seated in the place of honor beside God's throne. <sup class="verse-num">3</sup> Think of all the hostility he endured from sinful people; then you won't become weary and give up.

## The Story

The writer illustrates the spiritual journey by comparing it to an ancient athletic competition held in a packed stadium. The spectators are generations of believers who have already completed their own difficult paths. To compete effectively, the readers receive instructions to discard heavy cloaks and anything that might trip them up on the track. The winning strategy is to keep their eyes locked on the pioneer of their path, who successfully navigated the ultimate suffering and public disgrace. He pushed through the agony by focusing entirely on the profound gladness waiting at the finish line, establishing a perfect example for exhausted runners.

## Application for Today

We often accumulate heavy emotional and spiritual baggage as we navigate our daily routines. Resentments, anxieties, and destructive patterns can wrap around our ankles, making every forward movement feel like a struggle through deep mud. This passage invites us to take an honest inventory of our burdens and intentionally drop whatever drains our momentum. When the road stretches out endlessly and our energy fades, we are given a specific direction to look. Instead of relying purely on our own fading willpower, we can draw fresh stamina from the one who already conquered the hardest course. His resilience becomes the quiet fuel we need to simply take the next step.
