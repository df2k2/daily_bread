## The Reading (NLT)

**1 Peter 5:6-11**

> <sup class="verse-num">6</sup> So humble yourselves under the mighty power of God, and at the right time he will lift you up in honor. <sup class="verse-num">7</sup> Give all your worries and cares to God, for he cares about you. <sup class="verse-num">8</sup> Stay alert! ## The Story

The early followers of Jesus lived on the margins of their society, facing relentless social pressure and scattered hostility. Peter writes to these vulnerable communities to offer a framework for survival and endurance. He acknowledges that they are under genuine threat, describing an adversary that preys on the isolated and the weary. Instead of promising an immediate rescue from hardship, he points them toward a posture of radical humility. He reminds them that their suffering is not an isolated incident, but a shared experience among believers everywhere. The ultimate reassurance is that the timeline of their pain is short compared to the enduring foundation God is building beneath them.

## Application for Today

It is exhausting to carry the weight of our own survival, especially when we feel surrounded by threats to our peace. We often try to manage our anxieties by gripping tightly to control, forgetting that we were never meant to sustain ourselves. This passage invites us to release our grip and hand our deepest worries over to a God who is intimately invested in our well-being. It also serves as a stark reminder to stay awake, as cynicism and despair are predatory forces looking to consume our hope. When we stand firm together and acknowledge our shared human struggles, we make room for God to renew our strength. Ultimately, it is divine grace, not our own effort, that places our feet on unshakeable ground.
