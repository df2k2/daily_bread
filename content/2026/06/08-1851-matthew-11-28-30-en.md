## The Reading (NLT)

**Matthew 11:28-30**

> <sup class="verse-num">28</sup> Then Jesus said, Come to me, all of you who are weary and carry heavy burdens, and I will give you rest. <sup class="verse-num">29</sup> Take my yoke upon you. Let me teach you, because I am humble and gentle at heart, and you will find rest for your souls. <sup class="verse-num">30</sup> For my yoke is easy to bear, and the burden I give you is light.

## The Story

In the ancient world, agricultural imagery was a common way to explain spiritual truths. Teachers of the law often spoke of a specific yoke, representing the obligations and interpretations a rabbi expected his followers to carry. By the first century, this had become a crushing weight for many everyday people, as religious leaders demanded a level of performance and purity that was nearly impossible to maintain. People were left exhausted, feeling that they could never measure up to the divine standard. Into this culture of spiritual burnout, Christ offers a radically different path. He presents himself not as a taskmaster demanding perfection, but as a gentle teacher offering relief from the endless treadmill of religious striving.

## Application for Today

We still carry weights that were never meant for our shoulders. Even in our spiritual lives, it is easy to treat faith like a ladder we must climb through constant effort, correct knowledge, or flawless behavior. But the invitation here is to lay down the exhausting need to prove our worth. Taking on this new way of living means learning the unforced rhythms of grace from someone who is fundamentally gentle. When we stop trying to earn our place through spiritual performance, we finally discover the deep, sustaining quiet that our souls have been craving all along.
