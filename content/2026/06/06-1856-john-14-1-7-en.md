## The Reading (NLT)

**John 14:1-7**

## The Story

The disciples are sitting in the shadow of impending grief. Their rabbi has just washed their feet, predicted a betrayal from within their own ranks, and announced he is leaving them to go somewhere they cannot immediately follow. Panic is the natural response as the future they had imagined dissolves right in front of them. In the middle of this rising dread, Jesus addresses their anxiety head-on. He does not offer a detailed itinerary or a strategic plan for the days ahead. Instead, he offers a relationship, assuring them that their ultimate destination is secure. When Thomas voices the collective confusion of the room by asking for clear directions, the response shifts the focus entirely from a geographical location to a living person.

## Application for Today

When our own plans unravel, it is easy to feel entirely lost. We want a map, a set of coordinates, or at least a timeline to show us how we will navigate the uncertainty. We often pray for clarity about the future, asking for the exact steps we need to take to ensure our safety. Yet the comfort offered in this moment is not a step-by-step guide to avoiding pain. The comfort is the steady presence of the guide himself. We are invited to trust that the path forward is not a puzzle we have to solve, but a person we are called to follow. Even when the road ahead is completely obscured, we do not need to know the entire route if we are walking closely with the one who holds our future.
