## The Reading (NLT)

**Proverbs 3:5-8**

> <sup class="verse-num">5</sup> Trust in the Lord with all your heart; do not depend on your own understanding. <sup class="verse-num">6</sup> Seek his will in all you do, and he will show you which path to take. <sup class="verse-num">7</sup> Don't be impressed with your own wisdom. Instead, fear the Lord and turn away from evil. <sup class="verse-num">8</sup> Then you will have healing for your body and strength for your bones.

## The Story

The wisdom literature of ancient Israel often presents life as a journey along a winding path. It acknowledges a fundamental human struggle: our deep desire to figure everything out on our own. The writer understands that human intellect, while a beautiful gift, has limits and can easily lead to arrogance or anxiety when we rely on it exclusively. True wisdom begins with humility and a shift in our internal focus. By releasing our tight grip on our own logic, we make room for a deeper, more profound guidance. The promise here is not a detailed map of the entire journey, but simply the clarity needed for the next step.

## Application for Today

We often exhaust ourselves trying to calculate every possible outcome before making a move. This passage invites us into a different way of living, one marked by radical reliance on divine direction rather than our own exhaustive planning. When we stop trying to be the ultimate architects of our lives, a profound physical and spiritual relief follows. The tension literally leaves our bodies when we accept that we do not have to hold all the answers. Welcoming God into our daily choices brings a steadying, peaceful rhythm to our days. We are free to step forward in quiet confidence, trusting the One who sees the entire landscape.
