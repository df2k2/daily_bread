## The Reading (NLT)

**Psalm 121**

> <sup class="verse-num">1</sup> I look up to the mountainsdoes my help come from there? <sup class="verse-num">2</sup> My help comes from the Lord, who made heaven and earth! <sup class="verse-num">3</sup> He will not let you stumble; the one who watches over you will not slumber. <sup class="verse-num">4</sup> Indeed, he who watches over Israel never slumbers or sleeps. <sup class="verse-num">5</sup> The Lord himself watches over you! The Lord stands beside you as your protective shade. <sup class="verse-num">6</sup> The sun will not harm you by day, nor the moon at night. <sup class="verse-num">7</sup> The Lord keeps you from all harm and watches over your life. <sup class="verse-num">8</sup> The Lord keeps watch over you as you come and go, both now and forever.

## The Story

Psalm 121 is traditionally understood as a song of ascents sung by pilgrims traveling upward toward Jerusalem. The journey was often treacherous and filled with steep climbs, harsh weather, and the constant threat of bandits hiding in the rocky hills. When the psalmist looks toward the horizon, the towering peaks represent both the destination and the present danger. Instead of finding reassurance in the landscape itself, the traveler remembers the Creator of that landscape. The song shifts from a solitary question of survival to a communal declaration of divine vigilance, reminding the weary traveler that their ultimate security rests not in the safety of the road but in the unending attentiveness of God.

## Application for Today

We all face stretches of life that feel like an exposed uphill climb. The obstacles looming ahead can easily dominate our vision and make us wonder how we will ever find the resources to keep moving. This ancient prayer invites us to adjust our focus. Our security does not depend on a perfectly safe path or our own ability to anticipate every hazard. Instead we are invited to trust in a God who remains fiercely attentive to our reality, offering a steady and protective presence through the brightest days and the darkest nights. We can step forward with confidence knowing that the One who crafted the universe is intimately invested in our daily journey, guarding our every departure and arrival.
