## The Reading (NLT)

**Colossians 3:12-17**

> <sup class="verse-num">12</sup> Since God chose you to be the holy people he loves, you must clothe yourselves with tenderhearted mercy, kindness, humility, gentleness, and patience. <sup class="verse-num">13</sup> Make allowance for each other's faults, and forgive anyone who offends you. Remember, the Lord forgave you, so you must forgive others. <sup class="verse-num">14</sup> Above all, clothe yourselves with love, which binds us all together in perfect harmony. <sup class="verse-num">15</sup> And let the peace that comes from Christ rule in your hearts. For as members of one body you are called to live in peace. And always be thankful. <sup class="verse-num">16</sup> Let the message about Christ, in all its richness, fill your lives. Teach and counsel each other with all the wisdom he gives. Sing psalms and hymns and spiritual songs to God with thankful hearts. <sup class="verse-num">17</sup> And whatever you do or say, do it as a representative of the Lord Jesus, giving thanks through him to God the Father.

## The Story

In the ancient Mediterranean, clothing instantly communicated a person's identity, profession, and loyalties. The early believers in Colossae were a mixed group of individuals learning how to exist as a single family. To guide them, the letter's author uses the metaphor of getting dressed to illustrate how character is formed through daily, deliberate choices. Adopting virtues like compassion and humility is presented not as an automatic change, but as an active habit. The final piece of this spiritual wardrobe is love, acting as the crucial binding that keeps the diverse community from falling apart.

## Application for Today

Every morning requires a decision about what to wear, yet we rarely apply that same careful thought to the demeanor we present to the world. Extending patience and making room for the flaws of others takes real effort, especially in moments of friction. However, recognizing our own need for grace makes it far easier to offer it to those around us. The harmony described here functions like an umpire, settling disputes and keeping relationships intact when tensions rise. As we move through our ordinary routines, we have the opportunity to let everything we do reflect the deep gratitude we carry.
