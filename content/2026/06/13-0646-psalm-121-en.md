## The Reading (NLT)

**Psalm 121**

> <sup class="verse-num">1</sup> I look up to the mountainsdoes my help come from there? <sup class="verse-num">2</sup> My help comes from the Lord, who made heaven and earth! <sup class="verse-num">3</sup> He will not let you stumble; the one who watches over you will not slumber. <sup class="verse-num">4</sup> Indeed, he who watches over Israel never slumbers or sleeps. <sup class="verse-num">5</sup> The Lord himself watches over you! The Lord stands beside you as your protective shade. <sup class="verse-num">6</sup> The sun will not harm you by day, nor the moon at night. <sup class="verse-num">7</sup> The Lord keeps you from all harm and watches over your life. <sup class="verse-num">8</sup> The Lord keeps watch over you as you come and go, both now and forever.

## The Story

The ancient pilgrims traveling to Jerusalem faced treacherous routes. Their roads wound through steep, rocky terrain where bandits often hid, making the surrounding heights a source of deep anxiety. When the writer of this ancient song scans those peaks, the question of survival is very real. Yet, instead of finding reassurance in the landscape or their own travel preparations, they look beyond the physical world entirely. They recognize that true safety belongs to the one who engineered the peaks and valleys in the first place. The song shifts from a solitary traveler's anxiety to a communal declaration of profound trust in a Creator who never abandons his post.

## Application for Today

We still navigate landscapes filled with hidden dangers, whether they take the form of financial instability, health crises, or relational fractures. It is incredibly easy to scan the horizon for a quick rescue or to rely completely on our own contingency plans. This passage invites us to shift our gaze higher. The assurance offered here is not that the road will be entirely free of rocks or dark valleys, but that we are never walking it unobserved. The one who set the stars in motion is intimately attentive to our daily departures and arrivals. We can rest in the quiet confidence that our ultimate well-being is held by hands that never tire.
