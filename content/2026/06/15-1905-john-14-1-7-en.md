## The Reading (NLT)

**John 14:1-7**

If this were not so, would I have told you that I am going to prepare a place for you? <sup class="verse-num">3</sup> When everything is ready, I will come and get you, so that you will always be with me where I am. <sup class="verse-num">4</sup> And you know the way to where I am going. <sup class="verse-num">5</sup> No, we don't know, Lord, Thomas said. ## The Story

The disciples are sitting in a heavy, tense atmosphere. They have just finished a meal where their teacher washed their feet, predicted a betrayal from within their own ranks, and told them he is leaving for a place they cannot immediately follow. Their world is unraveling, and their immediate future looks terrifyingly uncertain. In the middle of this rising panic, Jesus speaks directly to their deep anxiety. He does not offer them a map or a detailed timeline of what will happen next. Instead, he offers them a relationship and a promise of ultimate belonging. Thomas, voicing the collective confusion of the room, asks for concrete directions, but the answer he receives shifts the focus from a geographic destination to a living person.

## Application for Today

When our circumstances become unpredictable, our first instinct is usually to demand a detailed plan. We want to know exactly what is going to happen, how it will be resolved, and the precise steps to get there. But the comfort offered here suggests that peace does not come from having all the answers or a perfectly predictable future. Peace is found in knowing the one who holds the future and trusting that there is a secure place prepared for us. When the path ahead feels obscured by worry or sudden changes, we are invited to stop frantically searching for a map. We can anchor our restless thoughts in the character of the one who walks alongside us, finding our direction and our rest in him.
