## The Reading (NLT)

**Proverbs 3:5-8**

> <sup class="verse-num">5</sup> Trust in the Lord with all your heart; do not depend on your own understanding. <sup class="verse-num">6</sup> Seek his will in all you do, and he will show you which path to take. <sup class="verse-num">7</sup> Don't be impressed with your own wisdom. Instead, fear the Lord and turn away from evil. <sup class="verse-num">8</sup> Then you will have healing for your body and strength for your bones.

## The Story

The book of Proverbs functions as a collection of generational wisdom originally framed as a parent passing down life lessons to a child. In the ancient Near East, survival often depended on deep communal knowledge rather than isolated individual guesswork. The writer contrasts the limits of human intellect with the vast and reliable perspective of the divine. Relying solely on our own intellect is depicted as a fragile foundation. True wisdom begins with humility and a conscious decision to release our grip on what we think we know. Acknowledging God is not just a religious exercise but an orientation of the entire life toward a deeper reality.

## Application for Today

We often spend our days trying to figure out every detail of our futures, building elaborate mental maps to keep anxiety at bay. Yet our personal perspective is always limited by our own experiences and blind spots. This passage invites us into the quiet relief of releasing that burden. It asks us to bring God into the ordinary moments and decisions rather than relying exclusively on our own calculations. When we stop trying to outsmart our circumstances and choose humility instead, we often find a profound sense of relief. That shift from intense self-reliance to gentle surrender brings a deep and restorative peace to our weary minds and bodies.
