## The Reading (NLT)

**Matthew 5:1-12**

> <sup class="verse-num">1</sup> One day as he saw the crowds gathering, Jesus went up on the mountainside and sat down. His disciples gathered around him, <sup class="verse-num">2</sup> and he began to teach them. <sup class="verse-num">3</sup> God blesses those who are poor and realize their need for him, for the Kingdom of Heaven is theirs. <sup class="verse-num">4</sup> God blesses those who mourn, for they will be comforted. <sup class="verse-num">5</sup> God blesses those who are humble, for they will inherit the whole earth. <sup class="verse-num">6</sup> God blesses those who hunger and thirst for justice, for they will be satisfied. <sup class="verse-num">7</sup> God blesses those who are merciful, for they will be shown mercy. <sup class="verse-num">8</sup> God blesses those whose hearts are pure, for they will see God. <sup class="verse-num">9</sup> God blesses those who work for peace, for they will be called the children of God. <sup class="verse-num">10</sup> God blesses those who are persecuted for doing right, for the Kingdom of Heaven is theirs. <sup class="verse-num">11</sup> God blesses you when people mock you and persecute you and lie about you and say all sorts of evil things against you because you are my followers. <sup class="verse-num">12</sup> Be happy about it! Be very glad! For a great reward awaits you in heaven. And remember, the ancient prophets were persecuted in the same way.

## The Story

Crowds were gathering, looking for a revolutionary leader or a quick fix to their oppression under Rome. Instead of rallying a military force or outlining a traditional path to power, the teacher retreats to a hillside and assumes the traditional seated posture of a rabbi. The vision laid out here completely subverts the cultural expectations of the day. Rather than honoring the wealthy, the powerful, or the victorious, this teaching elevates those at the very bottom of the social and spiritual ladder. It frames vulnerability and marginalization not as curses, but as the exact places where divine presence is most profoundly encountered.

## Application for Today

We spend so much of our lives trying to climb upward, seeking security, influence, and comfort. Yet this hillside teaching invites us to stop striving for the world's definition of success. When we find ourselves grieving, exhausted, or simply aware of our own deep spiritual poverty, we are not failing. Those moments of emptiness are precisely where we are invited into a different kind of reality. We are called to extend mercy, work for peace, and maintain integrity even when it costs us our reputation. True flourishing happens when we align our hearts with this quiet, enduring way of living.
