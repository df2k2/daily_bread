## The Reading (NLT)

If God is for us, who can ever be against us? <sup class="verse-num">32</sup> Since he did not spare even his own Son but gave him up for us all, won't he also give us everything else? <sup class="verse-num">33</sup> Who dares accuse us whom God has chosen for his own? No one for God himself has given us right standing with himself. <sup class="verse-num">34</sup> Who then will condemn us? No one for Christ Jesus died for us and was raised to life for us, and he is sitting in the place of honor at God's right hand, pleading for us. <sup class="verse-num">35</sup> Can anything ever separate us from Christ's love? Neither death nor life, neither angels nor demons, neither our fears for today nor our worries about tomorrow not even the powers of hell can separate us from God's love. <sup class="verse-num">39</sup> No power in the sky above or in the earth below indeed, nothing in all creation will ever be able to separate us from the love of God that is revealed in Christ Jesus our Lord.

## The Story

Paul was writing to a community in Rome that knew exactly what it meant to feel vulnerable. The early church lived under the constant shadow of political suspicion, economic hardship, and the very real threat of physical harm. In this letter, he addresses a deep, universal fear: the anxiety that suffering might be a sign of divine abandonment. He builds a courtroom scene where all accusations fall flat because the judge is already on the side of the defendant. Then, he pans out to a cosmic scale, listing every conceivable force in the universe. His conclusion is a sweeping, definitive rejection of the idea that anything could ever sever the bond between the Creator and the created.

## Application for Today

It is incredibly easy to interpret our personal hardships as evidence that we have been forgotten. When the diagnosis is bad, when the bank account is empty, or when relationships fracture, a quiet voice often whispers that we are on our own. Yet this passage insists that our circumstances are not a reliable thermometer for divine affection. The love described here is not a fragile sentiment that shatters under pressure, but a resilient anchor holding firm in the darkest depths. You do not have to earn this security, and you cannot accidentally lose it. Whatever is looming on your horizon today, it does not have the power to cut you off from the love that holds the universe together.
