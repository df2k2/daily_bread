## The Reading (NLT)

**Romans 12:1-2**

> <sup class="verse-num">1</sup> And so, dear brothers and sisters, I plead with you to give your bodies to God because of all he has done for you. Let them be a living and holy sacrifice the kind he will find acceptable. This is truly the way to worship him. <sup class="verse-num">2</sup> Don't copy the behavior and customs of this world, but let God transform you into a new person by changing the way you think. Then you will learn to know God's will for you, which is good and pleasing and perfect.

## The Story

Paul's letter to the Romans pivots dramatically at this moment. After spending chapters laying out the vast, unearned mercy of God, he finally turns to how ordinary people should respond. In the ancient world, worship almost always involved bringing an animal to an altar. Paul takes that familiar image and turns it inside out, asking his readers to offer their very lives, breathing and active, as the ultimate act of devotion. He acknowledges the intense pressure they face to blend in with the cultural rhythms of the empire around them. Instead of merely trying harder to be good, he points them inward, suggesting that true change begins with a complete overhaul of how they perceive reality.

## Application for Today

We are constantly being molded by the environments we inhabit. The demands, anxieties, and relentless pace of modern life act like a mold, pressing us into shapes that often leave us exhausted and disconnected. True spiritual transformation requires us to actively resist those external pressures and invite a deeper kind of renewal. It is a daily practice of unclenching our grip on the habits and assumptions that weigh us down. As we allow our thoughts and priorities to be gently realigned, the fog begins to clear. We start to recognize the path forward, discovering a way of living that feels deeply grounded, whole, and undeniably good.
